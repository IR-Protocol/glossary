# Time Capsule 01: Drawing a Dividing Line Between the Memory Chain Trilogy and Cognitive Intelligence

*Today is June 24, 2026.*

Over the past two months, we have published a series of white papers one after another. Many people, following the timeline, easily arrive at an impression: "Oh, this company published the Memory Chain white papers first, and then published something called Cognitive Intelligence."

And then what? Cognitive Intelligence gets treated as an "incidental chapter," framed within the Memory Chain's structure.

This is wrong. Today we are publishing this Time Capsule to make a most basic and most important distinction:

The Memory Chain and Cognitive Intelligence are two parallel series. They were designed simultaneously from day one. They only appeared sequentially when each reached its own milestone.

## Series One: AI Memory Chain

It addresses the "relations of production" and "asset ownership" in the AI era.

The opening (April 13) — *AI Memory Chain Commercialization White Paper*: It pointed out that subscriptions have reached their end. The essence of subscription is a relic of the "era of scarce computing power," treating AI as software to be sold. But AI is becoming infrastructure. Fixed monthly fees cannot keep up with floating compute costs. Platforms lose money, users feel anxious, and data ownership is unclear. Together, they form the "Triple Paradox [IR-TP-012]." This white paper defined a new concept: the AI Memory Chain [IR-MC-001] — the "relationship chain" of the AI era. The conversation history, preference habits, and project context accumulated between users and AI are not scattered data fragments, but a digital bond that can accumulate, be taken away, and continue across platforms.

The path (May 15) — *AI Memory Chain Commercialization White Paper 2.0*: It provided a path framework from "naming" to "exploration." It proposed Cognitive Anchoring [IR-CA-001] — mapping new concepts to existing human cognition (for example, comparing "storage-compute decoupling" to "separation of rent and electricity") to lower the threshold of understanding. It found a reference system validated over decades — China's telecom industry. Billions of users have already been educated in the logic of "monthly rent to keep your number, usage-based billing for traffic." It also proposed the technical concept of Auditability [IR-AUD-002] — making the AI's decision process traceable and verifiable, providing a technical interface for users' right to know and platforms' self-certification.

The terminal (June 8) — *AI Memory Chain Commercialization White Paper 3.0*: It answered "what each person's intelligent terminal looks like after the infrastructure is built." It proposed the vision of the Second Brain [IR-SB-001] — a personal AI assistant that accumulates knowledge structure, preference patterns, and experience, continues seamlessly across devices, and understands you better the longer it is used. It also gave the four standards of a True Exclusive AI [IR-TEA-002]: portable (complete export), unreadable (blind storage encryption), unbreakable (compute can be cut, memory never dies), and accountable (traceable decisions). With this, the Memory Chain series completed its loop from "naming" to "path" to "terminal."

Core objective: to turn AI from a "use-and-discard tool" into a "digital companion that belongs to you for life."

## Series Two: Cognitive Intelligence

It addresses the "nervous system" and "behavioral proportion" of the AI era.

Published (May 27) — *Cognitive Intelligence White Paper*: It proposed a core judgment — the stronger the large model, the more it needs a scheduling hub that understands proportion. Compute is muscle. But without a matching nervous system, the more developed the muscle, the harder it is to precisely control behavior.

To this end, it defined two concepts. **Cognitive Architecture (CA) [IR-CA-007]** — the top-level design of "what core capability modules a trustworthy AI system needs," containing three major modules: precise execution, contextual awareness, and independent judgment. **Cognitive Operating System (COS) [IR-CA-008]** — the scheduling design that lets these capabilities operate in coordination within an actual system, responsible for multi-modal perception fusion, dynamic resource allocation, and flexible expression generation.

It answers a question that current large models generally cannot answer: "What is the situation right now?" Can it detect when a user's voice is trembling? When emotional collapse and a technical inquiry appear at the same time, does it have arbitration capability? Should comforting a bereaved person and checking the weather involve a world of difference in compute allocation?

Core objective: to let AI learn "proportion" in the digital world — knowing when to act, to what extent, and in what manner.

## As of today

We have two parallel series, each supported by multiple pieces of content. One manages "money, memory, belonging." The other manages "brain, proportion, safety."

In June 2026, Intention Resonance Technology is exploring in two directions: one toward "memory sovereignty," one toward "cognitive safety."
