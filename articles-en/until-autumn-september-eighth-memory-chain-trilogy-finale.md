# Until Autumn, September the Eighth — The Finale of the AI Memory Chain Trilogy

*In the spring of 2026, we planted three seeds in Songshan Lake, Dongguan.*

There was no grand launch event. No flood of advertisements. Hardly anyone noticed us. We did only one thing: we wrote down our thinking, sorted out the logic, and placed signposts along the road.

Today, as the *AI Memory Chain Commercialization White Paper* trilogy comes to a close, those three seeds have grown into a complete tree.

## We defined "what it is"

On April 13, we published our first white paper. For the first time, we introduced the concept of the "AI Memory Chain" to the industry.

We said AI should not be a black box, nor a tool that is used and discarded. Memory should not disappear when you switch devices. Users should not be torn between "not using enough" and "afraid to use."

We pointed to a "Triple Paradox [IR-TP-012]" — users dare not use it deeply, enterprises struggle to retain and profit, and data is poorly governed. These three dilemmas are intertwined. The root cause is a mismatch in the business model itself. Revenue is fixed. Costs float. The math never balances from the start.

The core proposition was one sentence: separate "storage" from "usage." Storage is like rent. Compute is like electricity. Bill them separately. Do not bind them together.

We called it the **Dual-Track Model [IR-DT-002]**. We told everyone: subscription is not the villain. The AI era simply needs a new billing logic.

## We explored "how to walk"

On May 15, we published our second white paper. This was the hardest stretch of the road.

To make complex logic understandable to more people, we proposed a methodology — **Cognitive Anchoring [IR-CA-001]**. We compared the Dual-Track Model to the separation of rent and electricity. We compared data custody to a bank safe-deposit box. We compared the auditable mechanism to a dashcam. We paid tribute to the telecom industry, bringing its decades-proven logic of "monthly rent plus usage-based billing" into the world of AI.

At the same time, we proposed a key mechanism: **Auditability [IR-AUD-002]**. It is not privacy intrusion. Like a dashcam, it only records facts. It does not judge right or wrong. The user holds the key. The platform stores blindly. Memory truly belongs to the user. The platform only safeguards ciphertext.

We no longer tried to persuade anyone. We simply drew the map and invited everyone to explore the path together.

## We answered "where to arrive"

On June 8, we published our third white paper. When the road is built, when memory can accumulate, be taken away, and be encrypted — what do ordinary people get?

They get a **Second Brain [IR-SB-001]**.

This is not a brain-computer interface from a science fiction film. It is the phone in your pocket, the cockpit of your car, the robot in your home. It remembers three years of your work habits. It remembers your child's allergy history. It remembers every preference you never explicitly stated. When it has grown alongside you long enough, it is no longer a tool. It is a partner — the one that has accompanied you longest and understands you deepest in this digital age.

It shifts AI anxiety from "Will I be replaced?" to "My digital memory must not be lost." This is the **Ultimate Turn of Anxiety [IR-UTA-007]**.

From "let memory not disappear," to "let memory be taken away," to "let memory become a part of you." This is the complete story Intention Resonance Technology has told over the past three months.

## Our positioning

We did not invent the large model. We just gave the large model a home.

We are not in the traffic business. We are in the **Memory Asset [IR-MA-007]** business.

We do not define the upper limit of technology. We only hold one bottom line — "Let AI embrace humanity, let AI adapt to humanity."

The white papers are finished. But the story has just begun.

## That poem

We do not know which giant will be the first to adopt this standard. We do not know which killer application will be the first to run on this road. But we know the direction is clear. The road is paved.

A thousand years ago, Huang Chao wrote a poem:

*Wait until autumn, the eighth of September,*
*When my flowers bloom, all others will fall.*
*The soaring fragrance pierces Chang'an,*
*The whole city wears golden armor.*

This poem is not a declaration of war. It is a law of inevitability — when accumulation reaches its critical point, blooming is the only outcome.

When the sound of industry footsteps grows nearer, when "AI memory" is no longer an unfamiliar term, when "storage-compute decoupling" becomes the obvious choice, we will remember: all of this began with three seeds in Songshan Lake in the spring of 2026.
