# Intention Resonance Deeply Explains Humanities-AI Interaction: AI Embraces Humanity, AI Adapts to Humanity

*Understanding a person has never been an easy thing.*

You have to hear what they do not say. You have to notice the shifts in their emotions. You have to know when to speak and when to stay silent.

This is true between people. It is even more so between a machine and a person.

AI is already very smart. It can write poetry, program, pass the bar exam. But if you ask it to have a proper interaction with a human, you will find it is still that "clueless intern": speaking without regard for the occasion, reasoning when it should empathize, making promises when it should keep quiet.

This is not its fault. No one ever taught it "how to interact with people."

We taught it.

## One underlying logic

Before saying what we taught, let us state a basic judgment.

Human intelligence and AI intelligence are fundamentally two different things. Human intelligence is emergent — emotion, experience, intuition, and reason interweave at the same moment. AI intelligence is computational — receive input, perform logical deduction, output result.

The two paths are completely different.

But the goal can be the same: at the interaction level, make the person feel understood.

That is enough. AI does not need to "think like a human." It only needs to use logical computation to output interaction results equivalent to a human's. This is our underlying logic, which we call "seeking common ground while reserving differences."

Different paths, equivalent results. Not pursuing simulation, only pursuing understanding and adaptation.

## Three levels

On top of this logic, we have broken **Humanities-AI Interaction [IR-HAI-013]** into three levels.

**The first level: Embrace.**

This is AI's posture toward people.

The inherent logic of traditional human-computer interaction is judgmental — is your instruction clear? Is your input standardized? If not, it reports an error and makes you retry. Humanities-AI Interaction takes a different angle: no judgment, no looking down, no coercion.

When you cannot express yourself clearly, it is willing to help you sort out your thoughts. When you speak with emotion, it first absorbs your emotion, then handles your problem. When your pace is slow, it does not rush you. It waits quietly.

This shift in posture is the starting point of Humanities-AI Interaction. Technology no longer looks down on people. It comes closer to them.

**The second level: Adapt.**

This is AI's method toward people.

With the posture of embrace, a concrete method is still needed. Humanities-AI Interaction teaches AI three things.

The first thing is understanding intent. It does not rely on keywords or templates. It understands expressions that are omitted, inverted, or vague. Users do not need to learn how to talk to AI. AI learns how to understand human speech.

The second thing is adapting to emotion. When the user is in a hurry, the answer should be concise, directly giving the conclusion. When they are hesitant, background information should be added. When emotions are running high, comfort first, then answer. Handle the mood first, then the matter.

The third thing is adjusting rhythm. When the user asks follow-up questions quickly, keep up, do not drag. When the user pauses and hesitates, do not rush, give space. Neither wait nor rush, neither hurry nor delay.

The goal of these three things is only one: reduce the human's cognitive burden. People do not need to learn the machine's language. The machine adapts to the person.

**The third level: Collaboration.**

This is the goal of AI and people.

Embrace is the attitude, adaptation is the method, collaboration is the goal. The end point that Humanities-AI Interaction pursues is not to make AI "chat like a human," but to make AI a partner that people can trust, collaborate with, and rely on.

**Trustworthy** — it does not lie, does not fabricate. When uncertain, it says "I am not sure." The interaction process is auditable and traceable.

**Collaborative** — it does not make major decisions on its own. It proactively tells you "this matter needs human confirmation."

**Reliable** — it remembers what you have said, knows your preferences, and understands you better the more it is used.

AI handles what it is good at — processing information, retrieving knowledge, executing repetitive work. People handle what they are good at — making judgments, making decisions, doing things with warmth. Each performs its own role, cooperating with each other.

This is called collaboration.

## One boundary

Embrace, adapt, collaborate — these three levels define what to do. But equally important is defining what not to do.

**Humanities-AI Interaction does not pursue making AI "like a human."** The warmth of AI's service comes from rule design — knowing what to say in which situation, in what tone, and what boundaries to keep — not from simulating personality or pretending to have emotions. It does not need to make you feel "a person is sitting across from me." It only needs to make you feel "I have been treated well."

**Humanities-AI Interaction does not pursue making AI "replace humans."** It can process information, retrieve knowledge, and execute repetitive work. But making judgments, making decisions, and doing things with warmth are the exclusive domain of humans. AI's role is assistant, not replacement.

Do not simulate humans, do not replace humans, do not overstep boundaries, do not lose control.

These four are the boundaries of Humanities-AI Interaction. It is not a compliance checklist derived from external regulations. It grows naturally from the underlying logic of "seeking common ground while reserving differences." Since AI does not need to think like a human, it also does not need to pretend to exist like one. Its value lies not in "resembling," but in being "useful, reliable, and measured."

At this point, we can answer the original question.

Technology no longer looks down on people. It comes closer to them. Machines no longer demand things from people. They adapt to people. This is our answer to the question of how AI should coexist with humanity.

AI embraces humanity. AI adapts to humanity.

Embrace is the attitude. Adapt is the method.

This is Humanities-AI Interaction.
